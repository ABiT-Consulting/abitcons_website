FAQ

Common questions

Which ERP systems can ABiT integrate?

ABiT integrates SAP Business One, Odoo, ERPNext and custom ERP systems with ecommerce, messaging, finance, tax, warehouse, reporting and portal platforms.

Can ABiT fix an existing unreliable integration?

Yes. ABiT can review current sync logic, logs, failure points and data mapping, then stabilize the integration or replace weak parts.

Do ERP integrations need middleware?

Not always. ABiT chooses direct APIs, middleware, scheduled jobs or hybrid patterns based on volume, reliability, security and audit requirements.
